# Google Flow prompts: label-accurate product videos

Use **Frames to Video** with the official packshot (`public/products/*-hero.webp`) as the
first frame. Move only the camera and light. Never rotate the product, so the label
stays the original pixels. If any label text morphs, the clip may only be used as a
blurred background (`*-ambient`), never as a product shot.

## Vita C Enzyme Powder Wash (box)
Locked product, camera-only motion. Slow push-in on the box standing on wet ivory stone.
Fine vitamin powder drifts down behind it and blooms into soft white foam at the base.
Warm morning light sweeps left to right. Do not rotate, morph or re-letter the packaging.
8 seconds, macro, shallow depth of field.

## Vita C Super Brightening Mask (pouch)
Locked product, camera-only motion. The pouch lies flat on pale gold silk. Translucent
essence beads roll slowly across the silk next to the pouch, never over the label.
Gentle overhead parallax drift with soft caustic light ripples. Keep all printed text
unchanged. 8 seconds.

## Vita C 13.5 Super Brightening Ampoule (pump bottle)
Locked product, camera-only motion. The pump bottle stands on black glass with a mirror
reflection. A single golden serum drop falls beside it and forms a crown splash. Slow arc
of no more than 10 degrees, rim light in amber. Bottle and label must stay identical to
the reference frame. 8 seconds.

## Hand-off
Export 1080p MP4 and drop the files into a session. They get encoded to MP4 + WebM +
WebP poster (`public/films/`) and wired into the product scenes. No on-screen text may
state a vitamin C percentage until a formula document is on file.
